About the job
About Quantrue
We're a forward-deployed AI engineering firm that partners closely with clients to build and deploy tailored AI solutions. We focus on transforming complex business challenges into practical, production-grade technology that generates measurable impact. We emphasize co-development, working side by side with client teams to accelerate innovation and delivery.
Team members join a fast-moving environment where applied AI, engineering excellence, and business outcomes intersect.
Role description
As a Forward Deployed AI Engineer at Quantrue, you will work directly with clients to design, build, and deploy AI-driven software solutions that address real-world business problems. On a day-to-day basis, you will collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to understand requirements, prototype and implement backend services, integrate AI components, and ensure systems are robust, scalable, and maintainable. You will contribute to end-to-end development, from data processing and model integration to API design, testing, and monitoring in production environments.
You will also participate in architectural discussions and knowledge sharing to support sustainable, high-quality engineering practices.
This is a full-time hybrid role based in Madrid, with a mix of on-site collaboration (either at our client's offices our ours) and flexibility for work-from-home.
